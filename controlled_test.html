Controlled GPU benchmark
Fixed camera, gl.finish() sync, no interaction during test
Method: Each model is rendered for 15 seconds at a locked camera position. Every frame calls gl.finish() which forces the CPU to wait until the GPU has finished drawing before measuring the time. This gives a true, GPU-inclusive frame time, not just how fast the CPU can submit commands.

Controls: Camera and scene are identical for both tests. No orbit controls, no interaction. Both models are pre-loaded and centered before timing starts. First 60 frames are discarded as warmup.
