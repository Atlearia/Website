# Data collection notice

**Last updated:** April 4, 2026

## What this app does

Math Practice is a free arithmetic drilling tool. With your consent, it tracks your practice sessions anonymously so you can see how you're improving over time.

## What we collect (when you consent)

If you opt in, we store:

- A random anonymous ID generated by your browser. It's saved locally and not linked to your name, email, or any account.
- Practice data: which problems you tried, whether you got them right, and how long each one took. This is what drives the accuracy and speed stats.
- A one-way hash of your network address. We never store your actual IP. The hash lets us count unique visitors and group sessions, but can't be reversed back to your IP.

## What happens if you decline

Nothing gets stored. No anonymous ID, no practice data, no hashed IP. The app runs entirely in your browser with no server communication. You can still use it — you just won't have progress tracking between visits.

## What we don't collect

- Your name or email
- Browser fingerprints, ad identifiers, or cookies
- Your location
- Data from other sites or apps

## How long we keep data

Practice attempts are automatically deleted after 90 days. The anonymous user ID persists to let returning visitors continue tracking progress, but can be deleted on request.

## Who has access

Only the site administrator, who logs in with a secret key. There are no third-party analytics, no advertising, and no data sharing.

## Your rights

Under Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25), you can:

- Access the data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Request your data in a portable format
- Withdraw your consent at any time
- Request de-indexation

## How to withdraw consent

You can withdraw consent from the app footer, or from the in-app data collection notice page at `#privacy`. This removes your anonymous ID from your browser and stops all data collection going forward.

You can also clear your browser's local storage for this site.
